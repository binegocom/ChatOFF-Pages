Frequently Asked Questions (Sıkça Sorulan Sorular)

Does ChatOFF work without an internet connection?

Yes. ChatOFF bundles an on-device neural language model and runs 100% offline, including in Airplane Mode. No account or internet connection is required.

Where are my conversations and documents stored?

All conversations, local memory facts, tasks, and AI Studio files are stored exclusively on your iPhone or iPad inside the local SQLite database. Nothing is uploaded to the cloud.

How do I switch the app interface language?

Open the sidebar or Settings inside ChatOFF and choose from 9 built-in languages: Turkish, English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, Kurdish (Kurmanji), or Kurdish (Sorani RTL).

How do I export a project created in AI Studio?

Inside AI Studio, open the Export ZIP tab and tap Export to save or share the complete source archive via the native iOS Share Sheet.

Accessibility Support / Erişilebilirlik

ChatOFF is designed in accordance with Apple Human Interface Guidelines for inclusivity across iPhone and iPad:

  • VoiceOver & Voice Control: Semantic labels on all navigation, chat, task, and studio controls.
  • Larger Text (Dynamic Type): Layout verified up to 200% text scaling without clipping or overflow.
  • Dark Interface & Sufficient Contrast: High-contrast Light and Dark Cupertino themes.
  • Differentiate Without Color Alone & Reduced Motion: Clear iconography, status labels, and respectful motion curves.
