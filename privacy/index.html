Privacy Policy / Gizlilik Politikası

Effective Date / Yürürlük Tarihi: November 19, 2026 • Application: ChatOFF (com.binego.chatoff)

1. Summary — Zero Personal Data Collection (Özet: Sıfır Veri Toplama)

EN: ChatOFF is architected from the ground up as an offline-only, on-device artificial intelligence application. We do not collect, transmit, store on external servers, sell, or share any personal data, chat messages, prompts, voice recordings, documents, or usage analytics.

TR: ChatOFF tamamen çevrimdışı (offline) ve cihaz içi çalışacak şekilde tasarlanmıştır. Sohbet mesajlarınız, ses kayıtlarınız, belgeleriniz, görevleriniz veya kişisel verileriniz hiçbir uzak sunucuya gönderilmez, toplanmaz, satılmaz veya üçüncü taraflarla paylaşılmaz.

2. On-Device Processing & Local Storage (Cihaz İçi İşleme ve Yerel Depolama)

  • AI Inference: All language model reasoning is executed locally on your device's processor (Apple Silicon CPU / Metal GPU) using embedded LiteRT-LM and GGUF neural network weights.
  • Local Database: Conversations, personalized memory entries, tasks, and AI Studio projects are stored exclusively inside your device's application sandbox in a local SQLite database (chatoff_chat.db).
  • Data Deletion: You can delete individual conversations, memories, tasks, or projects inside the app at any time, or uninstall the app to permanently erase the local sandbox from your device.

3. Optional System Permissions (İsteğe Bağlı Cihaz İzinleri)

ChatOFF requests iOS system permissions only when you explicitly trigger a feature that requires them:

  • Microphone & Speech Recognition (NSMicrophoneUsageDescription / NSSpeechRecognitionUsageDescription): Used strictly for push-to-talk on-device voice dictation. Audio is never uploaded to any external server by ChatOFF.
  • Calendars & Reminders (NSCalendarsFullAccessUsageDescription / NSRemindersFullAccessUsageDescription): Used only when you explicitly approve syncing an extracted task to your local Apple Calendar or Apple Reminders via EventKit.
  • Files & Photo Library (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription): Used only when you explicitly select a local file or image via the system picker to import or export on your device.

4. No Third-Party Analytics or Accounts (Üçüncü Taraf Analitik ve Hesap Yoktur)

ChatOFF requires no user registration, email address, phone number, or account login. There are no cloud chat APIs, telemetry trackers, or crash-harvesting SDKs monitoring your conversations.

5. GDPR, Swiss revDSG & Apple App Store Compliance

Because all data remains strictly on your physical device under your sole control, no cross-border data transfers take place. In accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (revDSG), and Apple App Store Privacy Guidelines, you retain 100% ownership and physical custody of your data at all times.
